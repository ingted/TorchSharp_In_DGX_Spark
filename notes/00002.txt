• 你這個錯誤其實是「腳本硬編碼舊路徑」，還沒進到 CMake 就先 cd 失敗了。

  我已幫你改好，改成預設使用腳本所在目錄：

  - build_TorchSharp.Native.sh
  - build_TorchSharp.net.sh
  - publish_local_nuget.sh
  - pack.sh

  另外在 native 腳本加了 CLEAN_CMAKE_CACHE=1 開關，會清掉舊 CMake 中間目錄（TorchSharp/
  bin/obj/arm64.Release/Native）讓路徑重算。

  你現在直接跑：

  cd /workspace/home/qwen3.fs.experiments/TorchSharp_In_DGX_Spark
  CLEAN_CMAKE_CACHE=1 bash build_TorchSharp.Native.sh

  如果你要手動清再跑也可以：

  rm -rf /workspace/home/qwen3.fs.experiments/TorchSharp_In_DGX_Spark/TorchSharp/bin/
  obj/arm64.Release/Native
  bash /workspace/home/qwen3.fs.experiments/TorchSharp_In_DGX_Spark/
  build_TorchSharp.Native.sh
